About DESIGNRUN

DESIGNRUN — Civil & Structure Solution provides structural design, analysis and consultancy for residential, commercial and industrial projects — from concept to completion, built with precision.

All engineering deliverables are prepared and reviewed by qualified professionals against the applicable design codes.

Engineering Services

Structural Design & Detailing RCC and steel frame design, foundation schemes, member sizing and construction-ready detailing drawings.
Wind Load Analysis (CFD) Wind environment studies, cladding pressure guidance and comfort checks using validated CFD workflows.
RCC & Steel Design (RCDC) Design coordination and output review using industry-standard design tools and applicable codes.
3D Modeling & BIM (Revit) Coordinated 3D models, drawings and material quantity take-offs for better site execution.
Consultancy & Technical Support Design reviews, value engineering inputs, third-party checks and site technical support.
Project Intake & Requirements Structured requirement gathering through the DESIGNRUN Design & Analysis page.

How We Work

  • Understand the architectural intent, site conditions and applicable design codes.
  • Prepare structural schemes, analysis models and design basis notes.
  • Coordinate with architects, MEP consultants and contractors.
  • Issue drawings and calculations for review by qualified professionals.
  • Support construction through clarifications and site queries.

Final design outputs must always be verified by a qualified engineer against the relevant local codes before construction.
